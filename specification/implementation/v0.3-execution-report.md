## What this phase actually cost, and why

The two water issues were nominally S and M. The M was where the work was, and none of it was about
water: it was about discovering that **v0.2's terrain tuning had never applied at all.**

1. **`set_mapgen_setting(..., false)` means never, not "unless set".** The engine writes its own
   defaults into `map_meta.txt` at world creation, before mods load, so the third argument cannot do
   what its name suggests for a new world either. v0.2 shipped five inert settings and cited an
   elevation range of 19 as proof they worked — that number was stock v7 noise. All five are now
   `true`.
2. **v7's height is a blend, not a noise.** It short-circuits to `terrain_alt` when alt exceeds base,
   and otherwise interpolates base and alt through `height_select`. Tuning base alone moves almost
   nothing, which is the second reason v0.2's numbers refused to budge no matter what was set.
   `height_select` is now pinned to a constant and `terrain_alt` held below base, leaving
   `terrain_base` as the single dial — a pocket world needs one number a designer can turn, not three
   interacting ones tuned for an endless continent.

Both are in [docs/decisions.md](../../docs/decisions.md); the first explicitly supersedes what the
v0.2 report claimed.

## What the trees taught the gate

Adding trees turned the gate red at `surface_top_pct=78` — and the terrain had not changed. The probe
defined "the surface" as the first solid node from the sky, which, once trees existed, was a
**canopy**. The world was being reported as a fifth leaves.

Ground and canopy are now different things: the ground scan steps past leaves and trunks, and trees
are counted separately by their trunks. The definition is written down where it is computed, because
a later change that let a canopy back into `surface_top` would silently alter what every assertion in
the gate asserts.

This is the second time in v0 that a measurement's *definition*, not its threshold, was the defect —
after v0.2's post-release fix where a map-wide threshold was asserted against a fraction of the map.

## Architecture impact

No seam changed: `NODES` gained a row, which is what the table shape exists to allow.

One correction to record for later phases: **mapgen settings must be registered with
`override_meta = true`.** Any future phase touching mapgen that copies v0.2's pattern will produce
code that looks right, runs without error, and does nothing.
