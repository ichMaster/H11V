# v0.3 — GitHub Issues

Issues for phase **v0.3 — Water and trees** (version **v0**), derived from [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)
§v0.3 and the contracts in [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) (§Three details the engine is strict
about, §Engine primitives). IDs continue: H11V-008 → **H11V-009…012**.

The island stops being a bare green lump. Water gets its flowing partner and somewhere to actually
be, and trees arrive as a decoration schematic so they stay out of the walking-and-digging path.

**Reconciled against the real implementation (Step 0.5).** Three facts from v0.2's shipped code and
its review shape this phase:

- **There is no water in the world today.** The gate reports `water=0` — not because water is
  mis-registered but because the terrain's base offset is 6 while `water_level` is 1, so the whole
  island sits above the water line. The DoD says "water is present"; that is a *mapgen* problem, not
  a node problem, and it gets its own issue rather than being smuggled into the node work.
- **v0.2's review finding #1 has no regression test yet.** `water_source` was registered with a
  drawtype that does not exist and the engine silently drew a solid cube; it was invisible precisely
  because no water generates. This phase's assertions are that regression test.
- **`water_source` already names `water_flowing`** in `liquid_alternative_flowing`. The new row must
  close the loop in both directions, or the engine has a one-way reference.

## v0.3 — Water and trees

### H11V-009 — `water_flowing`: the liquid's other half

**Description:**
One row in `NODES`, and the engine stops having a one-way reference. Touches
`games/h11v/mods/h11_world/nodes.lua`.

**What needs to be done:**
- A `water_flowing` row: `drawtype = "flowingliquid"`, the flow animation on `special_tiles`,
  `liquidtype = "flowing"`, `paramtype2 = "flowingliquid"`, and a sensible `liquid_range`.
- **Cross-reference both ways.** `water_source` already names `water_flowing`; this row must name
  both itself and the source, or the pair is only half-linked.
- Use the drawtype the engine actually has. v0.2's review found `liquidsource` silently falling back
  to a normal cube; [ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md) pins `liquid` and `flowingliquid`, and
  those are the two names that exist.
- `h11_water_flowing.png` is the ordered texture ([ART.md](../ART.md) §5.1), 16 frames.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:** nine registered nodes, and a liquid pair the engine considers complete.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Nine nodes register; `h11_world:water_flowing` among them.
- [ ] Both nodes name both nodes in `liquid_alternative_source` / `liquid_alternative_flowing`.
- [ ] **Gate:** `check_lua.sh` and `test_worldgen.sh` green; no engine errors.

### H11V-010 — Terrain that meets the water line

**Description:**
Give the water somewhere to be. Touches `games/h11v/mods/h11_world/mapgen.lua`. Today's terrain
generates entirely above `water_level`, so the world has lakes and a sea in principle and none in
fact. The DoD asks for water to be *present*, which is a question about terrain shape.

**What needs to be done:**
- Adjust the base terrain noise, the water level, or both, so the 128×128 map has genuine water
  bodies — hollows that fill, and a shoreline where sand shows.
- Keep the elevation range the v0.2 gate asserts (≥ 8 at the contract area). Lowering the whole
  island is not a fix if it flattens it.
- Keep the mapgen settings registered as **defaults, not overrides**, for the reason v0.2 recorded:
  an existing world must keep the parameters it was created with, or v0.7's fixed-seed measurements
  drift between runs.
- Check the shoreline visually once in a Mac run: the biome's `node_riverbed` is sand, and a lake
  with no sand reads as a hole with water in it.

**Dependencies:** H11V-009

**Expected result:** the gate reports a non-zero water count, and the elevation assertion still holds.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `water > 0` on the 128×128 gate run.
- [ ] `elevation_range >= 8` still holds at the contract area — water did not cost the horizon.
- [ ] Digging a shoreline block produces flowing water, not an unknown node. (This is the real
      regression test for v0.2 review finding #1.)
- [ ] **Gate:** `test_worldgen.sh` green.

### H11V-011 — Trees as a decoration schematic

**Description:**
Trunks and leaves, placed by the engine at generation time. Touches
`games/h11v/mods/h11_world/mapgen.lua`. A Lua-table schematic passed to
`core.register_decoration` keeps trees entirely out of the walking-and-digging hot path — they are
generated once with the chunk, not maintained.

**What needs to be done:**
- A schematic: a trunk column of 4–5 `h11_world:trunk`, a leaf blob of `h11_world:leaves` around and
  above it, with the corner leaves marked as probabilistic so every tree is not identical.
- `core.register_decoration` with `deco_type = "schematic"`, placed on `h11_world:turf`, with a fill
  ratio tuned to give a wooded island rather than a forest or a lawn.
- The leaf blob must not be solid: `allfaces_optional` on the leaves node (already set in v0.2) only
  helps if light can get through the canopy.
- No `leafdecay` ABM in v0 — that is per-tick work, and v0 exists to measure the device with the
  simple case first.

**Dependencies:** None

**Expected result:** at least 20 trees in the 128×128 area, standing on turf.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] `trees >= 20` on the gate's 128×128 run.
- [ ] Trees stand on turf, not floating and not buried.
- [ ] **Gate:** `test_worldgen.sh` green; no engine errors (a malformed schematic logs one).

### H11V-012 — Assert water and trees

**Description:**
Close the v0.3 DoD in the gate. Touches `tools/test_worldgen.sh` and `tools/worldgen_probe/`.

**What needs to be done:**
- The probe already counts `water` and `trees` at the surface. Trees are better counted as *trunks
  at the surface*, which is what it does; state the definition in a comment so a later change does
  not silently alter what the number means.
- Assert `water > 0` and `trees >= 20`, at the contract area only — the same reasoning as v0.2's
  post-release fix: these are properties of the 128×128 map, not densities.
- Verify each new assertion **red** once by breaking the thing it guards, then revert. A gate never
  seen to fail is not known to work.

**Dependencies:** H11V-010, H11V-011

**Expected result:** `test_worldgen.sh` proves the whole v0.3 DoD.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Asserts `water > 0` and `trees >= 20` at the contract area.
- [ ] Both verified red once, then reverted.
- [ ] Three consecutive runs agree.
- [ ] **Gate:** `test_worldgen.sh` green.
