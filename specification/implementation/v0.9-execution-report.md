## Detailed results

### H11V-019 — Gate repairs · `1f228aa`
Eight findings of one kind. `run_local.sh` aborted on every documented flag; `check_assets.py --pack`
audited the retired pack so the colony delivery of record could not be gate-audited at all;
`check_lua.sh` parsed with a Lua dialect the engine rejects; `install_assets.sh` exited 0 on a failed
downscale; `MIN_TREES=4` demanded 3.2× the DoD; `mktemp -t` killed the gate on the Pi.

**Proved rather than asserted:** M16 and L22 were each demonstrated against synthetic inputs the old
gate accepted and the new one rejects — a flattened colour-type-2 re-export of `h11_bloom.png`, and an
8 px node tree. L25, L26, M11 and M14 were likewise reproduced against the old behaviour first.

**Surfaced, out of scope:** `install_assets.sh --res=16` cannot install green (`h11_crate_side.png`'s
horizontal wrap breaks when halved) — not a regression, identical under the pre-fix gate. Recorded as
**N2**.

### H11V-020 — Credentials and the deploy · `fb61883`
The phase's security fix. Every `sshpass -p` moved to `-e`; the password now lives in the child's
environment (same-uid readable, like the credentials file it came from) instead of in rsync's
world-readable argv. The `env SSHPASS=… cmd` prefix shape was deliberately avoided — it would have put
the secret straight back into an argv.

Three checks that were not checking: the post-launch renderer confirmation could never fire (nothing
set `debug_log_level`); the config swap raced a dying engine after the SIGKILL escalation; and
`gpu_preflight` could pass on EGL evidence alone about a path the game never takes.

**Device not touched** — it was in use. Every path was verified statically and by executing the new
decision blocks against stubs.

### H11V-022 — The documentation pass · `eee6c7f`
Five HIGHs in two files. `CLAUDE.md` still declared the repository specification-only with no code,
and prescribed a video driver the device build refuses to start on. `ARCHITECTURE` §Components
described a pre-retheme catalogue — the list the same document tells a v1 issue-writer the mutation
rules address — while its own §Mutation section already assumed a block §Components did not know
existed.

**Architecture impact:** the drift lasted a phase because nothing reads prose, so the assertion is now
code. `check_assets.py` compares the ids named in §Components against the ids `nodes.lua` registers
and fails in either direction, and is **promoted to an every-issue gate** — measured at 1.02 s, and
four of five pipeline skills already ran it unconditionally. Proved red four ways (phantom id, deleted
id, renamed bullet, renamed heading), so it goes red rather than blind.

### H11V-024 — The art audit's three gaps · `9e08c97`
No PNG was touched; the audit was wrong, not the art. Every number was re-measured in CIELAB over
fully opaque pixels with a linear-light cross-check.

Re-measuring **changed the framing rather than adding two rows**: a pair inside 8 L\* is not a failure,
it is where Rule 2 applies. 24 pairs qualify, exactly 7 also fall inside 60° of hue — and they are not
the seven §11 listed. Two of the originals were never failures; two real ones were absent.

**Two outcomes differ from the recommendation.** `prefab` ↔ `regolith_side` is recorded **open for the
next re-delivery** with a measured 2.9 L\* lever, not fixed. And the review's proposed fix for Rule 5's
fourth pair was **refused on measurement**: +5 L\* would land `lithic_top` 0.5 L\* from `fines`,
trading a thin top/side step for two live Rule-2 failures inside one column.

**Found, unlooked-for:** **Rule 6 is unpassable as written.** At 30% brightness white reaches only
L\* 32.5, so above dimmed bedrock there is room for three rungs of 8 L\*, not six. Hue survives the
dimming almost exactly; chroma does not. This matters before v1.1 designs a biome against a binding
rule no six-material ladder can satisfy.

### H11V-021 — Game code · `0a145ef`
The mapgen's "one dial" was never the only dial: `mgv7_spflags` was unset, so v7's mountain and ridge
passes ran on, reading none of the three pinned noises.

**A/B'd on the same seed, 48 sites to radius 8000:** engine defaults put the highest surface at
**y = 179** with ridge-carved meltwater channels; pinned flags give **y = 31** and dry land. The
island's own surface runs 6–25, and `test_worldgen.sh` is byte-identical before and after — which is
the finding rather than a surprise, since the gate measures 128×128 and the world is unbounded.

`settle()` was measured over **all 6228** real spawn candidates around the origin: **1122 (18%) landed
on a bloom crown.** The naive fix regressed 225 candidates into the unsettled position, so the loop
keeps the topmost growth-top as a fallback. Final: 6003 on ground, 225 on a crown, 0 nowhere.

**A correction to the review:** `zoom_fov = 0` does **not** remove the magnifier on 5.10 — that
version's `touchcontrols.cpp` adds the button unconditionally. The property silences what the button
does; the button and its tap zone remain. M25 is therefore recorded **partial**.

## What v0.9 did that the review did not ask for

Four findings were surfaced by the work itself and are recorded as **N1–N7** in the review document,
all independently confirmed. The two worth naming here:

  `World 'h11v_dev' not available` and falls back to `worlds/world`: `--worldname` only *selects*, and
  `--world <path>` is the creating form. Found independently by two implementers. **The
  visible-change gate has been walking a world nobody created.**
- **N6** — `.claude/skills/execute-issues/SKILL.md` keeps `check_assets.py` conditional (it is now an
  every-issue gate) and names two pack paths, one of which has never existed at any commit.
